POLICY CODE: BANK-KYC-001
TITLE: Sample Bank Customer Due Diligence and Record Retention Policy
OWNER: Compliance Department
STATUS: SYNTHETIC DEMONSTRATION DOCUMENT

SECTION 1: PURPOSE
This policy describes sample procedures for customer identification,
transaction record retention, and regulatory information requests.

SECTION 2: TRANSACTION RECORD RETENTION
The bank shall maintain records of domestic and international customer
transactions for at least five years from the transaction date.

SECTION 3: CUSTOMER IDENTIFICATION RECORDS
The bank shall preserve customer identification and address records
for at least five years after the customer relationship ends.

SECTION 4: REGULATORY REQUESTS
The bank shall make customer identification records and transaction
data available promptly to competent authorities upon request.

SECTION 5: TRANSACTION RECONSTRUCTION
The bank shall maintain transaction details including transaction type,
amount, currency, date, and parties involved.

SECTION 6: RECORD RETRIEVAL
The bank shall maintain a system that permits authorized personnel
to retrieve required account information efficiently.
