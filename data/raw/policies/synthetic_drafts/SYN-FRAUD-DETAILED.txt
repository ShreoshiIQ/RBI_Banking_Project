TITLE: Payment Fraud Monitoring - detailed testing variant
OWNER: Payment Risk
STATUS: SYNTHETIC DEMONSTRATION DOCUMENT - UNREVIEWED DRAFT

SECTION 1: PURPOSE AND SCOPE
Fictional commercial-bank testing policy. This is not evidence of a real institution's controls or regulatory certification. Applicability remains unreviewed.

SECTION 2: OPERATING PROCEDURE
The fraud team reviews payment alerts, documents the investigation and customer contact, and routes suspicious events to the designated escalation owner.
The team tracks alert resolution, customer impact, detection failures, and corrective actions without treating an alert alone as proof of fraud.

SECTION 3: OVERSIGHT
The policy owner records review findings and assigns an action owner and target date. Implementation evidence is required separately.
